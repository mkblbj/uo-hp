## Business Activities

- **OEM, processing, and wholesale sales of smartphone cases for all device models**
- **Direct import, wholesale, and sales of domestic foods**
- **Cross-border coordination between Japan and China, including listings and production**
- **B2B wholesale and Japan-China trade**

## Business Structure

UO Co., Ltd. integrates **e-commerce operations, product planning, processing, wholesale sales, and cross-border coordination**. Customer understanding gained from real sales operations is connected to future product development and new business expansion.

## History

::: timeline 2012
**Starting point**  The founder joined a smartphone accessories trading company and built the foundation of the Japan-China supply chain.
:::

::: timeline 2015
**Market entry**  Opened an online store and began selling custom-made smartphone cases.
:::

::: timeline 2018
**Incorporation**  Established UO Co., Ltd. and began organized business expansion.
:::

::: timeline 2019
**Multi-store expansion**  Opened 3911, 十色生活, and other stores on Rakuten Ichiba.
:::

::: timeline 2021
**Breakthrough**  A single store exceeded JPY 100 million in sales and reached 31st among all Rakuten stores.
:::

::: timeline 2024
**Diversification**  Launched the foods business and the 国内産屋 project.
:::

::: timeline 2025
**Year of technology**  Completed in-house development and field implementation of AI management software.
:::

::: timeline 2026
**All-channel expansion**  Officially began B2B wholesale to major variety stores in Kanto.
:::
